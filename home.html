FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who can participate in the counselling?

Eligible candidates included in the management counselling process can participate using their registered student credentials.

What documents are required?

Candidates should keep the documents specified by the college ready before participating in counselling.

How does seat allotment work?

Counselling proceeds according to the assigned rank. When a candidate's turn arrives, available department seats can be selected according to the counselling rules.
